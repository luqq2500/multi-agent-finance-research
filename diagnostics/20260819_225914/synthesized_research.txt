### Interpretation and What Would Close the Gaps
As a foreign private issuer, Toyota reports director and executive compensation disclosures in Item 6.B of its annual Form 20-F filings. Because granular qualitative and quantitative performance weightings and individual target breakdowns are generally not mandated or disclosed in the same proxy-style format required of domestic U.S. issuers under Regulation S-K, specific policy details were not retrieved. For Tesla, proxy materials focus on advisory votes and the ratification of previously established performance-based stock options (such as the 2018 CEO Performance Award), lacking granular breakdown tables and explicit compensation committee formulas for opex or financial strength within the retrieved snippets.

To close these gaps, manual or targeted reviews of complete, unabridged SEC exhibits (such as Item 6.B of Toyota's full 20-F filings and comprehensive compensation discussion and analysis (CD&A) sections of Tesla's DEF 14A filings) would be required to extract exact incentive plan structures, assuming such granular metrics are publicly disclosed.
