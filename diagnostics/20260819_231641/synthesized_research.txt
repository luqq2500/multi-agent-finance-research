### Supporting Evidence

#### Toyota Motor Corporation
- **Aggregate Remuneration and General Compensation Structure:** Toyota discloses aggregate remuneration for its board members and executive officers in Form 20-F (Item 6.B). For the fiscal year ending March 31, 2016, aggregate remuneration (including bonuses) was approximately ¥2,333 million [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094517/000119312516630645/d19734d20f.htm]. For FY 2015, the aggregate amount was approximately ¥2,032 million [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094517/000119312515232464/d877884d20f.htm].
- **Bonus & Incentive Framework:** Members of Toyota's Board of Directors receive year-end bonuses approved at the general meeting of shareholders or by the Board in accordance with company policy [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094517/000119312516630645/d19734d20f.htm].
- **Board Governance:** Toyota separates management oversight (Board of Directors) from operational execution (operating officers and executive officers) [src: https://global.toyota/pages/global_toyota/ir/library/corporate-governance/corporate_governance_reports_e.pdf]. Its Basic Policy on Corporate Governance targets stable long-term growth of corporate value, maintaining positive stakeholder relations, and adhering to the Corporate Governance Code [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094517/000119312524167425/d70988dex991.htm].

#### Tesla, Inc.
- **DEF 14A Proxy Details & 2025 CEO Performance Award:** Tesla’s 2025 proxy materials include Proposal Four, requesting shareholder approval for the 2025 CEO Performance Award granted to Elon Musk [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000110465925087598/tm252289-4_pre14a.htm, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000119312518035345/d524719ddef14a.htm].
- **Stock Option & Restricted Stock Vesting Conditions:** The 2025 CEO Performance Award is structured such that the number of vesting shares is reduced in accordance with Section XI, consistent with the participant being compensated solely on share price appreciation after the grant date [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000110465925108602/tm2530188d1_ex4-4.htm].
- **Regulatory and Shareholder Approvals:** Issuance under the 2025 CEO Performance Award occurs on the later of the termination/expiration of the HSR Act antitrust waiting period and shareholder approval [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000110465925108602/tm2530188d1_ex4-4.htm].
- **Advisory Votes:** Proxy statements featured Proposal Two, covering the non-binding advisory vote approving executive compensation for FY2024 [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000110465925087598/tm252289-4_pre14a.htm].
- **Shareholder Value Alignment:** Disclosures from the State Board of Administration of Florida (SBA) and Tesla indicate the performance-based incentive structure is designed to drive long-term shareowner value by focusing on performance outcomes tied to share price growth [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000093807625000017/fsbateslase.htm].
