### Direct Answer

An analysis of capital expenditure (capex) initiatives across Tesla, Microsoft, Alphabet (Google), Meta, and Nvidia reveals distinct strategic divergences in how these technology giants deploy capital. While Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, and Tesla are heavily investing in AI infrastructure, data centers, and advanced compute—often putting short-term pressure on free cash flow—they frame these expenditures as essential moats for long-term competitive advantage. Conversely, comprehensive official quantitative metrics and line-item disclosures for Nvidia, alongside specific calendar-aligned capex figures for Alphabet, official annual 10-K capex tables for Tesla, and precise FY2023 figures for Meta, could not be fully retrieved under the strict grounding constraints.

### Key Findings by Company

#### 1. Microsoft
* **Capex Figures:** Additions to property and equipment were **$8,943 million** for FY2023 and **$13,873 million** for FY2024 (ended June 30, 2024) [src: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2024-q4/press-release-webcast]. Primary capex categories or line items isolated specifically to cloud artificial intelligence are not explicitly broken out as separate sub-categories in official 10-K property and equipment disclosures; totals reflect overarching investments in data centers, servers, networking, and related cloud/AI infrastructure.
* **Free Cash Flow & Capital Discipline:** For FY2024, cash flow from operations reached **$55.4 billion** (up 30%), and free cash flow was reported at **$19.6 billion** [src: https://www.roic.ai/quote/MSFT/transcripts]. Management noted that elevated capital spending required to bring AI and cloud capacity online created temporary headwinds for quarterly free cash flow (e.g., quarterly FCF reported at $15.8 billion in specific periods) [src: https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/MSFT/pressreleases/2052058/microsoft-earnings-call-highlights-ai-fueled-cloud-surge].
* **Competitive Advantage & Shareholder Value:** CEO Satya Nadella stated that Microsoft built platforms like Copilot and the Copilot stack to help customers turn AI into measurable outcomes. Executive VP and CFO Amy Hood highlighted that AI infrastructure and Azure investments drove Azure and cloud services growth (e.g., Microsoft Cloud revenue reaching $33.7 billion in Q2 FY24), supported by scaling paid seats such as exceeding 30 million paid seats for Microsoft 365 Copilot [src: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2024-q2/press-release-webcast, https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/microsoft-holds-line-infrastructure-spending-160257955.html].

#### 2. Meta Platforms
* **Capex Figures:** Meta reported **$37.3 billion** in capital expenditures for the 2024 fiscal year [src: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-sacrifices-near-term-cash-131659294.html]. 
* **Free Cash Flow & Capital Discipline:** Meta achieved operating cash flow of **$91.3 billion** and roughly **$54 billion** in free cash flow in 2024 [src: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-sacrifices-near-term-cash-131659294.html]. However, accelerated capital expenditure strategies directly compress near-term free cash flow as the company prioritizes long-term computing capacity. Furthermore, Reality Labs continues to operate at a massive loss, generating over $17.7 billion in operating losses while bringing in just over $2.1 billion in revenue [src: https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2024/q1/META-Q1-2024-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf; https://www.investing.com/analysis/meta-platforms-from-heavy-ai-capex-to-2026-roi-200673593].
* **Competitive Advantage & Shareholder Value:** Management commentary highlights that Meta is investing heavily in data centers, custom chips, and GPUs to build computing power for AI model training and core algorithms across its apps [src: https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2024/q1/META-Q1-2024-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf]. Management's thesis relies on these computing investments eventually driving superior user engagement, ad monetization, and competitive advantage.
