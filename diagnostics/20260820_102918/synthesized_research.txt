### Interpretation

* **Strategic Focus and Capital Allocation Differences**: 
  * *NVIDIA* leans heavily into forecasting macro-level demand, aligning its product lifecycles (such as Blackwell and Rubin) with hyperscalers' multi-trillion-dollar infrastructure buildouts. 
  * *AMD* reflects a capital allocation strategy focused on high-margin software, systems design, and product engineering (e.g., retaining rack-scale design talent from ZT Systems) while divesting heavy manufacturing infrastructure (such as selling ZT Systems' manufacturing unit to Sanmina). This structure minimizes direct manufacturing capital expenditures on heavy factory assets while scaling R&D for accelerators like the Instinct MI300 and MI350 series.
