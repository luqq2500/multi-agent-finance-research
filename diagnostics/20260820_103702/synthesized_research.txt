### Synthesist Interpretation & Gap Closure
The available evidence confirms that major technology providers aggressively escalated capital deployment through FY2024 to support generative AI, specialized cloud capacity, and custom silicon development. However, comparing these strategies directly is constrained by heterogeneous reporting periods (e.g., NVIDIA's fiscal year ending in January 2024 versus Microsoft's ending in June 2024, and calendar-aligned years for Tesla, Alphabet, and Meta) and variations in cash-basis versus commitment-basis capex metrics (prominently seen in Meta's gap between property additions and guidance ranges). Furthermore, Tesla's missing 10-K total consolidated capex figure and lack of itemized Dojo metrics prevent a fully balanced comparative quantitative assessment across all five entities. Closing these gaps would require standardized reporting frameworks isolating AI compute infrastructure from general corporate property, plant, and equipment additions across all filing types.
