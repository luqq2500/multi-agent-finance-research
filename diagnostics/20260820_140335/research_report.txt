# Business Strategies and FY2024 Performance Analysis: JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup, and Morgan Stanley

## Executive Summary
JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup, and Morgan Stanley each executed distinct business strategies in FY2024 centered on balance sheet fortification, technology modernization, and structural simplification. While JPMorgan Chase leveraged its #1 retail deposit and investment banking fee rankings alongside broad digital and branch investments [1, 3, 7], Citigroup pursued major structural transformations including organizational flattening and international consumer divestitures [5, 6], and Morgan Stanley advanced toward its long-term asset accumulation goals in Wealth and Investment Management [9]. Granular segment-level financial breakdowns remain incomplete across all three institutions based on the available filings.

* JPMorgan Chase reported firmwide net revenue of $42.8 billion (Managed Revenue: $43.7 billion), an 18% ROE, and a 22% ROTCE [2].
* Citigroup reported consolidated net revenues of $89.6 billion and consolidated net income from continuing operations of $21.2 billion [8].
* Morgan Stanley achieved $61.8 billion in net revenues, earnings per share (EPS) of $7.95, and an ROTCE of 18.8% [10].
* Morgan Stanley generated $18.0 billion in long-term net flows for the full year 2024 and closed the year with $7.9 trillion in combined client assets within Wealth and Investment Management [9, 10].

## JPMorgan Chase Strategy and FY2024 Performance
JPMorgan Chase focused its core operations across four main segments: Consumer & Community Banking (CCB), Corporate & Investment Bank (CIB), Commercial Banking (CB), and Asset & Wealth Management (AWM) [1, 4]. CCB served over 84 million consumers and 7 million small business clients, maintaining the #1 retail deposit share and #1 primary bank ranking for U.S. small businesses [1, 7]. CIB prioritized delivering a united Commercial & Investment Bank [3], with investment banking fees rising 49% year-over-year to secure a #1 ranking for Global Investment Banking fees with a 9.3% wallet share [2].

Firmwide net revenue was reported at $42.8 billion, with Managed Revenue at $43.7 billion [2]. The firm delivered an 18% ROE and a 22% ROTCE [2]. Capital allocation and strategic investments targeted technology infrastructure modernization, data centers, cloud migration, traditional AI/machine learning, and Generative AI to enhance software engineering, customer support, personalization, and fraud prevention [12]. Additional investments supported branch expansion and digital capabilities, with over 41 million customers visited a Chase branch in 2024 [1].

## Citigroup Strategy and FY2024 Performance
Citigroup organized its operations around five core segments: Services, Markets, Banking, Wealth, and U.S. Personal Banking (USPB) [5]. The bank's structural transformation featured organizational flattening and the execution of international consumer business divestitures, including sales in Poland and Mexico/Banamex, aimed at streamlining operations and resolving legacy regulatory and reporting complexities [6].

Consolidated net revenues reached $89.6 billion, and consolidated net income from continuing operations stood at $21.2 billion [8]. Strategic targets outlined a multi-year return target trajectory toward a 10% to 11% ROTCE alongside a $30 billion share repurchase authorization framework [13]. Core investment areas emphasized technology modernization, automation, artificial intelligence (AI), liquidity management, and blockchain-based digital assets/tokenized deposit networks [13, 14]. CEO Jane Fraser noted that the bank successfully rebuilt its engine by flattening the organizational structure and resolving tangled risk and reporting systems [13, 15].

## Morgan Stanley Strategy and FY2024 Performance
Morgan Stanley operated through three core segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management [11]. Wealth Management and Investment Management held $7.9 trillion in combined client assets at the end of 2024, contributing toward the firm's long-term goal of $10 trillion [9].

Net revenues totaled $61.8 billion, with earnings per share (EPS) of $7.95 and a return on tangible common equity (ROTCE) of 18.8% [10]. The firm generated $18.0 billion in long-term net flows for the full year 2024 [10]. Morgan Stanley's strategic trajectory is characterized by consistent and durable earnings, strong capital accretion, and business momentum across all segments [9].
