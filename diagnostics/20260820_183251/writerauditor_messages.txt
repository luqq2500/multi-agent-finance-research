Microsoft\'s Maia, Alphabet\'s TPUs, Meta\'s Broadcom-partnered custom ASICs, and Tesla\'s Samsung-produced AI5/AI6 chips serve as critical long-term deflationary offsets. It suggests that split-focus development (maintaining separate custom chip roadmaps for in-house supercomputers vs. on-vehicle and robotics inference engines) is too resource-intensive even for massive Capex spenders.
