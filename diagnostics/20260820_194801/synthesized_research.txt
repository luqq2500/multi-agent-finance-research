### Direct Answer

In Fiscal Year 2024, capital expenditure (capex) strategies across Tesla, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Alphabet (Google), and Meta Platforms focused heavily on scaling AI infrastructure, high-performance compute clusters, GPU procurement, and data center capacity. 

Official SEC filing cash flow statements retrieved show FY 2024 property and equipment additions of **$55,700 million ($55.7 billion)** for Microsoft [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000095017024087843/msft-20240630.htm] and **$31.0 billion** for Alphabet [src: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204425000014/goog-20241231.htm]. Tesla reported expected capex exceeding **$10 billion** in FY 2024 [src: https://www.marketwatch.com/story/tesla-expects-capex-to-exceed-10-billion-in-2024-4dace5e2], with retrospective summaries citing annual spending of **~$11.3 billion** [src: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/inside-teslas-20b-capex-plan-122200458.html]. For Meta, projections range from **$60 billion to $65 billion** [src: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/meta-spending-ai-facebook-data-centers-9452a88f], with reports citing **$64 billion** in data center, server, and network spending [src: https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/meta-capex-hits-64bn-on-data-center-servers-and-network-spend-costs-expected-to-grow]. Exact SEC Form 10-K cash flow line items for direct property and equipment purchases were not retrieved in available snippets for NVIDIA and Meta.

### Interpretation and Gap Resolution

#### Synthesist Interpretation
Across the five companies, capital spending strategies in FY 2024 reflect distinct focus areas:
1. **Cloud & Platform Providers (Microsoft, Alphabet)**: Microsoft ($55.7B) and Alphabet ($31.0B) scaled physical data center capacity and GPU compute to serve internal AI models (e.g., Gemini) as well as commercial enterprise cloud demand (Azure AI and Google Cloud).
2. **Consumer/Social AI Infrastructure (Meta)**: Meta's capex strategy focused on dense GPU cluster acquisitions (targeting 350,000 H100s / 600,000 H100-equivalents) and data center restructuring to train open foundational models (Llama) and power internal app features.
3. **Autonomous Robotics & Physical AI (Tesla)**: Tesla's capex strategy (~$10B+ / ~$11.3B) shifted toward custom data centers and GPU compute capacity dedicated to training Full Self-Driving (FSD) and physical AI systems.
4. **Enabler Ecosystem (NVIDIA)**: While internal property/equipment capex was not isolated in snippets, NVIDIA's management commentary underscores that its strategic role is driven by capturing the broader industry's multi-billion-dollar infrastructure buildout.
